Questions

Things people ask before booking

How long does an inspection take?

It depends on the size and type of property. A typical apartment takes a few hours; villas, commercial spaces and large plots take longer. We confirm the time when you book.

Do I need to be present?

It helps, because you can ask questions as we go, but it isn't required. If you're abroad or busy, join by live video call, or simply wait for the report.

What do I get at the end?

Same-day highlights on WhatsApp, followed by the full ThirdEye Report: photo-tagged findings, severity ratings, locations and a builder-ready snag list.

Can you inspect a new flat before handover?

Yes, and it's one of our most common requests. We audit every zone, then re-inspect after the builder completes repairs to verify each item is closed.

Is this a structural engineering certificate?

No. ThirdEye is an instrument-assisted inspection and documentation service. If we see signs of structural distress, we flag it clearly and recommend a structural engineer's opinion.

How is the fee decided?

By property type, size and service. Share the details on WhatsApp or the form below and we'll send a clear quote.
